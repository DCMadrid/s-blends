Blend Buddies – S Blends

Pick a word. S is friendly and wants to join the other letters. The adult slides S along its friend’s arm while the child holds “sssss”, then they link arms, light up the blend and say the word. Pair words (pin → spin) start without S. The listen slides at the end ask “which one did you hear?”.
